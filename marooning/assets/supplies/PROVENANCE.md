# Supply assets

Author: Avramania. Source: https://avramania.itch.io/props-tools-fencing
Free edition, **20 models (CC0)**, acquired through the standard free download on
2026-11-26. Individual archive License.txt confirms CC0 1.0 for models/textures.
Only six relevant GLBs are retained: sack, basket, pot, rope coil, axe and barrel.
Original free archive SHA256:
`cbad6055dae51b9e53f415a7a9e8ed7d2d298087768bbc9d85939f3d45417e3d`.

Modifications: runtime scale/origin normalization to 0.65m bounding size; instanced
shared imported meshes/materials; fruit added to basket; barrel represents water.
339–2348 triangles per model, tiny embedded palette texture. Original GLBs are
unchanged. No FBX, unused pack models, premium assets or paid downloads are included.
The machete, fishing kit, cup, tarp, blanket, bamboo, firewood and coconuts use
project-authored low-segment composed silhouettes in supply_visual.gd, with shared
materials and no textures. All visuals are independent of catalog IDs and utility.

The existing galleon/environment/character licenses are unchanged.
