## B. Personal canteens
Proposed tuning: 1000 ml capacity, initially full for fresh and migrated marooning seasons (one-time baseline, no fabricated past water or contribution, do not alter existing hydration/condition). Exact integer ml, actor-owned, cannot lose/borrow/steal. Distinct from hydration fixed point. Drink on safe land (not underwater, swimming, locked menus/dialogue), own canteen only. Sip up to 250 ml restores up to 12 hydration points proportional to actual ml (use deterministic integer arithmetic and cap at deficit; ceil needed volume, clamp result); no water/benefit when full hydration or empty canteen; no overspend. Same function NPC/human; command ownership/expected revision and trusted land state; no per-frame repeated calls. No free well hydration, no automatic drinking on refill. Keep legacy earned water as finite reserve, optional "Refill from camp reserve" at shelter: top up own canteen from actual existing stock, no tribe contribution, handle partial available, atomic; new well service never consumes this legacy stock. No new water containers or shared water production.
At existing well (spring anchor), two actions: Fill my canteen (4 real active seconds=16 ticks); Collect water for tribe (12s=48 ticks for 6, 16s=64 ticks for 9; fixed at start based on active tribe size, use <=6 vs >6 for future). Same shared well capacity 3; distinct activity mode within existing physical site, not separate capacity pools. Start only when actual relevant deficit; tribal action requires at least one other active member below capacity, otherwise offer personal fill. No instant reward; progress starts at actual arrival, only successful completion tops own/all active same-tribe canteens to capacity, abstract distribution to everyone regardless of physical location (explicit owner design), no hydration change until drink. Compute actual deficits at completion, no double credit if two simultaneous fills, zero benefit allowed after competing completion; deterministic order. Move away, cancel, conversation interruption, failure release reservation and grant no water/credit. Menu/background pauses exactly like v3; save/reload preserve remaining ticks and revalidate physical presence, resume if still at well or cancel without reward, no offline catch-up. AI restored traveling must re-arrive; preserve remaining duration including work/fire/trail and modes. Human input remains two-thumb; no forced new animations or camera. 
NPCs drink when appropriate (e.g. hydration <=75, canteen >0 and safe land; small decision-boundary action, no every-frame loop), seek well when low canteen/hydration. Choose personal vs tribal using actual deficits, time, workdrive, condition, capacity, seeded variation; no name-based scripts, no access to rivals' needs, no human bias. Use existing scheduler and attribute cache; same outcomes for physical and abstract camps. No fantasy stock generation by decorative tasks. Need-aware food interaction: human can select available coconut OR fruit at shelter, no consume at full nutrition, no raw rice. Preserve legacy v1 gameplay.
