Each linked page was independently opened. No new external model/source was
imported, downloaded, purchased or redistributed. Existing asset notices remain.

Selected: project-authored procedural bamboo raft in raft.gd/raft.tscn. Adjustable
3.9 × 8m platform, membership-derived seats, central cargo, lashings and lightweight
paddles. Source provenance is the implementation commit. No new external-asset
hashes or redistribution conditions apply because none were retained.

## Reference relevance

[AnimatableBody3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_animatablebody3d.html)
estimates moving-platform velocity. Explicit seated anchors avoid double application.
[CharacterBody3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_characterbody3d.html)
provides the script-driven collision/movement used by existing motors.

[Tay and Kong](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6341952/) is specifically a
video-based stroke synchronisation measurement study in crew sprint kayaking,
verified from retrieved title/metadata. A later full-text lookup hit a browser
check. It is timing-design inspiration, not evidence for bamboo-raft coefficients.

The supplied [Cambodia](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s31/calendar.htm) and
[Game Changers](https://www.truedorktimes.com/s34/calendar.htm) pages are accessible
season chronologies; no physical specifications are inferred from them. The
[CBS Palau page](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/first-impressions-on-palau/) did not
provide usable opening-mechanics detail in the retrieved excerpt and is not relied
on for implementation facts. No copyrighted article text is copied into the game.
