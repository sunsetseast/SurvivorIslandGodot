# Aquatic ecosystem — future 5F.3
## Direction
Living water, not RNG fishing button. Inspired by Andaman coast, gameplay rarity not scientific abundance. Attractive lightweight textured models, convincing swimming, species silhouettes and coloration; no 795k triangle scans in runtime. No need for exhaustive species count. Begin modestly with small school fish, medium reef fish, large solitary fish, small crab and occasional large crab, ambient noncatchable wildlife.

Habitat zones: shallows (small schools/crabs/net), rocky edges (medium fish/crabs), reef/seagrass (variety), offshore (rare large fish, diving risk). Zones persist across scene entry/load, seeded populations and bounded replenishment; captured entities removed; fish respond to movement and disturbance; same logical stock for all contestants. Rivals abstract equivalent population, no render cost. Visible schools shared mesh/material and lightweight motion, nearby catchable logical individuals have stable identities/positions, no decorative phantom catches. Rendering quality does not change catchability.

Candidate roster: anchovy/sardine-like small school fish, fusilier, rabbitfish, snapper, grouper, trevally, shore crab, occasional large swimming crab; lobster later; turtles/rays/butterflyfish/starfish/hermit crabs decorative. Species and safety/ecology details to validate when milestone starts. Food proportional to size, initial example equivalent meal portions 0.25/1/2/3/5/8/1/3 respectively, lobster 4; not final nutrition tuning. Six vs nine person tribes different needs.

## Equipment
Cast net: shore/shallow throw and retrieve, target area; no fish within actual cast coverage means zero; fish escape chance based on skill/dexterity/accuracy/behavior. Usually one small fish, occasional multiple/suitable medium catches; no generated catch from empty water. Hawaiian sling: equip, swim/dive, locate, aim/release, hit actual fish, retrieve and return to camp. Miss cannot generate catch. Player skill plus attributes affect stability/accuracy/effort. NPC uses equivalent actual opportunity/skill and gear reservations, no privileged invisible catches. No handline fishing. No conventional fishing without gear; shore crab/forage remains available.
Mask/snorkel/fins equip as one set; faster swim, further fish visibility, less swimming fatigue; can breathe only when snorkel opening reaches air near surface. Deeper dives require breath. Breath meter uses active REAL seconds (not 120x clock), capacity from endurance/condition, exertion influences drain, surface recovery, clear visual/audio warnings. Exhaustion triggers safe medical rescue to shore, interrupted action, meaningful time/health/energy penalty; no medevac until health/elimination system supports serious outcomes. NPCs surface intelligently and follow same rescue rules. No deep-water sling milestone before breath/rescue foundation.
Deliver catches to camp to stock/credit; preparation and cooking consumes fish. Providers matter socially if noticed; private catch/absence/gossip later evidence-based.
