# Survival activities design bible
## Principles
Physical 3D context, no generic grind. Time, energy, actual available materials, NPC parity, interruption and meaningful outcomes. Shared activity lifecycle and deterministic outcomes with trusted local presence; rival tribes use same semantic rules abstractly. No teleport/instant harvest, no rewards for selecting a task. Future manual and assisted modes for repeatable skill actions may share a resolution system; both cost time/resources; attributes matter without hardcoding contestants.

## Activities
Water (5B.1): personal canteen, drink from it anywhere safe on land, fill at well, longer tribal fill refills active members' canteens, positive objective team contribution only for actual water delivered to others. No relationship magic, no communal new water containers, no drinking from thin air. See exact spec.
Ground collection (5B.2): fallen wood around trees/deadwood, bamboo near groves, coconuts and fronds near/beneath palms; low quantities, deterministic habitat placement and replenishment, visible depleted nodes. Carry to camp, only delivery credits shared stock. No infinite reset by scene/reload. Basket increases yield/capacity later.
Active harvest (5B.3): more yield than floor collection. Reusable short timing action for chopping bamboo or larger fallen deadwood; no need to fell whole trees. Palm shaking uses skill/effort and real available loose fruits/fronds; not guaranteed. Climbing for skilled contestants derived from strength, dexterity, balance, endurance and condition; assisted animation first if feasible, no hardcoded Ozzy advantage, defer if animation blocked. Ground pickup remains accessible to everyone. Avoid exact future spawn/timing quotas until playtest.
Shelter (5F.1): cooperative persistent project, NOT a chopping-style minigame. Frame, sleeping platform, covered roof, reinforcement (0–4 visual stages). Incremental work preserved across interruption, consume actual materials only at defined construction commit boundaries. Distinguish productivity, quality, social outcome. Campcraft/strength/dexterity/endurance/energy/tool access influence work; laziness drives willingness; leadership, trust, affinity and behavior influence cooperation. People can succeed despite disliking each other or bond while building poorly. Emergent disagreement/lead/follow/mediate/teach choices, credit stealing only if observed, no scripted name rivalries. Rope improves stability, tools efficiency; tarp later protection. Existing legacy ShelterBuildRules.js has 25–88% binary odds using strength, rest, relationship, laziness, style; adapt inputs not binary roll. No Minecraft building. Physical progress visible; NPCs finish without player.
Fire/cooking (5F.2): friction ember, nurture, establish flame; flint reward simplifies ignition. Reusable skill mechanic for later firemaking challenge. Fuel, weather, maintenance, condition; no imaginary flame. Pots for rice/boiling, spit for roasting; prepare and share actual food; simple repeatable mechanics; private eating possible later with social consequences. Raw rice not edible. Coconuts modest hydration benefit when food system revisited.
Fishing/diving: see AQUATIC_ECOSYSTEM.md. Idol hunting (5H): real exploration, actual hiding places, no guaranteed progress bar, readable clue referring to environmental features, NPC parity, privacy, witness/absence/following. No idol markers or global knowledge.
Social work: shared activity invitations require consent and actual presence; conversations in work contexts later 5E, no automatic acceptance. Track objective quantities/effort now, perceived reputation later based on owned evidence; witness not automatically confirmed by broad proximity. No direct relationship bonuses yet.
